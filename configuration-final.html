A

Settings by section, with planned absences typed ahead

A plain settings page opened a few times a year, plus a weekly look at Absences. The six sections are Crews, Capacity, Absences, Standing orders, Reasons, and KPI targets and names. Each one is a table edited in place, and only editors can change it.

  1. Open Configuration. A gold mark on a section means something needs attention.
  2. Crews: each person's crew, whether they count in capacity, and the week that starts. The gold banner lists the people CATS_DA found with hours on maintenance orders and no crew. Assign them, or mark them "not maintenance".
  3. Capacity: 8 h per day, 5 days, overtime above 40 h, and which CATS types count as absence (PTO, HOLI) or as worked time (REG, CALO).
  4. Absences (new): add a row for a person with the type (PTO, HOLI or On-call) and the from and to dates, as soon as it is known, before anything is booked in SAP.
  5. A planned PTO lowers that crew's capacity on the week board and greys the person out on the daily plan. On-call is shown there as a marker and does not change capacity.
  6. After the week is booked, the Status column compares each row with CATS_DA: Planned, Booked in CATS, or Differs. When they differ, CATS wins for the KPIs and the week close flags it.
  7. Standing orders, Reasons, and KPI targets and names work the same way: edit a cell, then save.
  8. Every save asks for the week it starts. A closed week keeps the configuration it was closed with.
