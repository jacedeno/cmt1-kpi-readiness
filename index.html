Which SMRP indicators SAP can feed today

We are adopting the SMRP Best Practice Metrics as the name and definition of every maintenance KPI. This page checks, indicator by indicator, whether the data SAP ECC already exports is enough to compute it, what is partial, and what only the planning tool can provide.

Three manual steps. SAP Data Explorer exists today but only browses the backlog; it takes no part in the weekly cycle.

Last week · closed
  • KPI tab: personnel per crew, available hours, planned and executed hours, unplanned hours by reason.
  • Plan Attainment and Planning Rate computed by spreadsheet formulas.
This week · in transit
  • Actual hours typed per job and weekday, Monday to Thursday 9:30.
  • Emergency jobs added as "unplanned" rows.
  • Every morning: the daily plan for the day (one row per person, free text) is printed.
Next week · being built
  • The workbook is copied, the week changed, every job typed again.
  • Presented at the Thursday 10:00 planning meeting.

What the week-38 workbook showed: 40 of 94 jobs had no SAP work order, preventive orders never reached the file, the daily plan was not linked to the weekly rows, and a broken SUM range understated planned hours in the KPI tab. None of this is carelessness; it is what a copied spreadsheet does over 38 weeks.

To verify in SAP, besides SCHD Seven questions. The first one decides the most.

  1. Revision / user status / scheduled start. Can ZEMR031 or the IW39 layout of this role include them? If not, the freeze lives only in the tool until S/4HANA.
  2. Meaning of the Date column in ZEMR031 (creation, basic start or basic finish). It decides Aging, Overdue and PM Compliance.
  3. Work values on ZP01 task lists. Without them, Planned Work and Backlog ignore preventive work.
  4. Where PM time is posted: ZP01 orders or internal Y-orders. CATS_DA shows it.
  5. Attendance types SDOT, SNCE, CLND, CLNO, PTOB, UPML, UPFM, and the days with more than 24 h in CATS_DA.
  6. Contractors. Whether any SAP source (PO, service entry sheet) carries their hours; otherwise manual entry.
  7. Electrical and Reliability rosters. ZEME047 only lists the 13 mechanics.

About the standard. SMRP Best Practice Metrics (Society for Maintenance & Reliability Professionals, 6th edition) is the US reference and the basis of the CMRP certification. EN 15341:2019 is the European CEN standard. SMRP and EFNMS published jointly "Global Maintenance and Reliability Indicators", which maps 40 EN 15341 indicators to 36 SMRP metrics, so the codes on this page have a documented European equivalent.
