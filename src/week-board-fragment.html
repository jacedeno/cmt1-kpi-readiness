Week board: three ways to lay it out

Same information in all three (SAP orders, day, hours, capacity per crew, overtime). What changes is how the planner moves things. Examples use real week-38 jobs. Click the one that matches how you think about the week.

A

Backlog and week by day: drag each order to its day

Left: the SAP backlog with filters. Right: five columns, one per day, one card per order with its hours. On top, capacity per crew with overtime in gold. The most visual layout; each card moves between days with the mouse.
