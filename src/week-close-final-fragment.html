Chosen by the planning team on 25 Sep 2026: layout B's frozen vs actual table, with screen 1's filters and search to find any order, and a Feedback column per order. Example: week 38 (14–18 Sep), closed Thursday 24 Sep from the CATS_DA export of that morning.

B+

Frozen vs actual table, with search and a Feedback column

One row per order that was frozen or worked, with frozen hours, booked hours, status, a reason cell only where one is needed, and a free-text Feedback cell on every row. Above the table are screen 1's filters and a search box, so any order can be found without leaving the screen. The KPI figures sit on top and change as reasons are filled in.

  1. Upload the morning's CATS_DA and ZEMR031 closed exports. The table fills: frozen hours from the snapshot, booked hours per order from CATS_DA, and a status computed for each row (done, partial, not worked, break-in).
  2. Filter to "needs a reason". Break-ins and rows not worked show a dashed reason cell; pick from the list.
  3. Search by order number or equipment, or filter by planner group, priority or type, the same as on the week board.
  4. Write feedback on any order: a comment or a justification. It is stored with the closed week and shown when the week is opened from the KPI page.
  5. Check the rows flagged above the estimate (5.3.5). They need no answer; they are there to be seen.
  6. Review the lines below the table: overtime, contractor hours (typed by the mechanical supervisors) and differences between planned absences and CATS_DA.
  7. Type the headline and press Close week. The week stays visible as this table, and reopening it asks for a reason.
