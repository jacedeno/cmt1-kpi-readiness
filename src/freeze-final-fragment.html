Edit freely, check, freeze after the meeting; every later change on the ledger

On Thursday next week's plan is an editable table: rows can be written, deleted and added from the backlog. Layout A's checks run on every change. Red items block the Freeze button, gold items only warn. The planner makes the changes the room asked for, then presses Freeze. The lock is not taken in front of the group. Once frozen, the week keeps layout C's ledger underneath.

  1. Open next week (39) on Thursday. It holds what was planned on the week board, and the checks run at once.
  2. At the meeting, show the week as it is and note what the room asks for. Nothing has to be locked there.
  3. After the meeting, edit the table: change hours or days in a row (✎), delete a row (✕), or add one from the filtered backlog (+ Add order).
  4. Clear the red items: create the missing order or put its hours in SAP and re-upload, or delete the row. Gold items stay visible and do not block.
  5. Press Freeze. The week gets a lock with date, time and who; that snapshot is what 5.4.3 and 5.4.4 measure against.
  6. Until Sunday night, unfreeze and freeze again with a reason. From Monday, every addition is a break-in and every removal a deferral, each with its reason, recorded on the ledger that week close (screen 4) reads.
