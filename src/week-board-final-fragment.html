A+C

Filtered backlog on the left, crew lanes by day on the right

The backlog keeps layout A's filters and a search box. The week is three crew lanes (Corrective, Reliability, Electrical) by five days, as in layout C. Each cell shows its hours against that crew's capacity for that day, and planned absences lower the capacity before anyone is booked.

  1. Upload the ZEMR031 export; the backlog fills with the open orders.
  2. Filter by planner group, priority, type or order type, or search by order number or equipment.
  3. Drag an order onto "this crew, this day". A multi-day order is split across days.
  4. Watch the cells: gold means planned overtime, allowed and visible. Move jobs between days to level the load.
  5. Check the capacity bars on top for the whole week per crew.
  6. Planned PTO typed in configuration shows in the cell and lowers its capacity (Friday, Corrective: 72 → 64 h).
