A and C combined, with B's period selector

Chosen by the planning team on 25 Sep 2026: layout A's scorecard on top, with gauges where they fit; layout C's single-indicator trend below; and one period selector for both. Example: week 38 (14–18 Sep), closed Thursday 24 Sep. The eleven weeks before it, and every 12-week figure, are illustrative, not plant data.

A+C

Scorecard with gauges on top, one indicator's trend below

The page opens on the last closed week. Each SMRP code is a tile. A code with a target and a natural 0–100 % range gets a gauge, with a black tick at the target. Counts and hours keep plain numbers. Picking a tile fills the lower half with that indicator's trend, its formula and the orders behind the number. The period selector sets the range for both halves.

  1. Open KPIs. Week 38 is shown with its headline.
  2. Pick the period: 4 wk, 12 wk, 36 wk, 52 wk, YTD, or Custom (from week … to week …). Each tile keeps the last week's value on the gauge and adds the period's value underneath.
  3. Read the gauges. The arc is the week's value and the tick is the target; gold means close to the target, green met, red far. Grey means no target yet; targets are set in configuration (screen 6).
  4. Click a tile. The trend below switches to that code and shows every week of the period against its target. Hollow points are imported weeks, solid points are weeks closed in the tool.
  5. Read the formula box: the fixed SMRP formula with the week's numbers and the period's numbers. A period value comes from summed hours, never from averaging the weekly percentages.
  6. The gap list shows the frozen orders not worked or worked short, with their close reason and feedback. Each one opens the closed week (screen 4).

What was combined. From A: the scorecard of the week, one tile per code, now with a gauge where it fits. From C: the single-indicator trend with its formula and the orders behind the gap, opened from a tile. From B: only the period selector, extended to 4, 12, 36 and 52 weeks, year to date and a custom range. B's full history table is not a screen; its Excel export can come later if the monthly report needs it.

As before: the page only reads closed-week records and never lets a number be typed. Every value is computed from hours with the fixed SMRP formula, and labels are display aliases. A code a week never recorded is missing, not zero, so a 12-week value may cover fewer weeks, and the tile says which. Targets and aliases are set in configuration (screen 6). The history since week 40 of 2025 comes from the current KPI web app; in this mockup weeks 27–37 are illustrative.
