A+C

People first on screen, the detailed sheet on paper

The screen is layout A: today's jobs on the left, one row per person on the right. The left panel also opens the whole backlog with screen 1's filters and search, so a break-in can be picked from every open order. Each person line takes a note, and the hours of each assignment can be typed. Print turns the day into layout C's sheet.

  1. Pick the day. The crew that is in appears one row per person; people on PTO (planned in configuration, or booked in CATS_DA) are greyed out, not deleted.
  2. Left, today's jobs from the week board with the hours still to assign. Switch to Backlog to search every open order with screen 1's filters; an order added from there is a break-in (gold, dashed).
  3. Drag a job onto a person. It lands with the hours still open (up to 8 h); type over the hours to split a person's day (e.g. 6 h on one job, 2 h on another). This is the day's assignment only; the order's estimated Work stays in SAP.
  4. Each row adds up to 8 h. Below 8 h it shows "free hours" in red; above, planned overtime in gold.
  5. Write a note on the person's line: tools, permits, "with Cameron N.", "after lunch".
  6. Print: one page per crew in layout C's format, with person, equipment, WO number, description, job, hours and notes. The WO number is what the person books in CATS.
  7. Mid-day change: move the chip and print again. The tool keeps the last version of the day.
